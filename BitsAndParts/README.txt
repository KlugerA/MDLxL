BitsAndParts

Store reusable .mdl and .mdx models here. You can organize them into nested
folders, for example helms/horns. Keep each model's required texture files at
the relative paths used by that model.

In MDLxL, use the goblin button beside Forge to preview and import a whole part.
Press Refresh after changing files outside the app. Choose an animation,
geoset color, and sample frame if you want one source RGB applied to the part.

Imports use Forge's shared DummyBone and can be undone. Previewing or closing
the browser does not alter your model.
