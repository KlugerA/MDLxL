# Showcase rendering target

Showcase targets classic Warcraft III SD model presentation. It uses the project's WebGL2 `war3-model` renderer with MDLxL's shader adapter for animated normals, geoset tint and alpha, and the existing Warcraft material and texture sampling rules. Live recording follows elapsed wall time, including camera motion, while simulation advances in bounded steps. Encoder backpressure skips intermediate captures instead of slowing the preview. Actual frame timestamps determine the GIF delays; the final delay keeps the requested duration exact. GIF encoding happens after recording finishes. Local and global animation clocks stop when no selected sequence advances, including the 0% speed setting.

Portrait sequences use a separate portrait path. The model camera is evaluated at the current frame, the Human UI frame is composed around the render, and the portrait shader uses the classic white directional light with a 0.3 ambient floor. The direction used by this preview is `[0.3, -0.3, 0.25]`, following the coordinate conversion in the [Warsmash portrait light manager](https://github.com/Retera/WarsmashModEngine/blob/main/core/src/com/etheller/warsmash/viewer5/handlers/w3x/W3xScenePortraitLightManager.java) and its [default static light](https://github.com/Retera/WarsmashModEngine/blob/main/core/src/com/etheller/warsmash/viewer5/StaticSceneLightInstance.java). That is a researched reference implementation, not a claim that every Warcraft III patch uses identical portrait data.

The imported renderer documents support for standard geometry and animation, global sequences, layered alpha, texture animation, ParticleEmitter2, and partial RibbonEmitter behavior. It documents no Light-node or render-priority support; RibbonEmitter gravity and texture-slot/color animation are also outside its documented support. Legacy ParticleEmitter and ParticleEmitterPopcorn rendering are not provided by this renderer. MDLxL adds some SD shader behavior, but it does not supply all missing engine features. These limits mean Showcase is not a complete Warcraft III engine replacement. Check effect-heavy or light-dependent models in Warcraft III before treating a capture as an exact in-game result. See the upstream [`war3-model` renderer support list](https://github.com/4eb0da/war3-model/blob/master/README.md#renderer-support).

The quality presets change actual preview sampling and export settings. Low uses 1x sampling, no AA, bilinear mip filtering, 1x anisotropy, a 720px GIF edge and 1280px PNG edge. Medium uses 1.5x sampling, AA, trilinear filtering, 4x anisotropy, 1280px GIF and 1920px PNG. Highest uses 2x sampling, AA, trilinear filtering, up to 16x anisotropy, 1920px GIF and 3840px PNG. GPU support can limit AA and anisotropy. Lighting, geometry, particles and source textures remain the same at all qualities. PNG keeps full color; GIF is limited to 128 colors on Low and 256 on Medium/Highest.

Folder backgrounds load in Showcase. Image/Video accepts local images, animated GIFs and browser-decodable videos; video playback is muted and loops inside the From/To interval. WAV is audio-only and cannot supply a background picture. GIFs and videos animate during live recording. Screenshots seek the chosen video section to the sampled animation pose.

The compact left menu edits sequences in Add/right-click dialogs. Record entries play in list order and overflow is marked red. Camera paths use a shared duration with speed, straight/arc curve and smoothness controls. Orbital mode is exclusive with free camera paths and completes one orbit about the model bounds center over the entire recording. Screenshot batches sample distinct animation poses and Frontal, Orbital, Zoom or Free cameras, with 1-20 takes per animation. Each entry saves into a separate animation-screenshots-UUID folder inside the model folder.
